Parking Booking & Management: Smart Corporate Parking Solutions

September 29, 2026 6 min read

Parking might seem like a mundane facility issue, but for large corporate campuses, it's a daily pain point that affects employee experience, productivity, and even retention. The morning scramble for parking spots, conflicts over assigned spaces, visitor parking challenges, and the growing need for EV charging—these issues demand a smart, integrated solution. Shiksha Infotech's Parking Management platform transforms corporate parking from a daily frustration to a seamless experience.

The Parking Problem

Corporate parking challenges are multi-dimensional:

  • Limited Supply: Fewer parking spots than employees who drive to work
  • Unfair Allocation: First-come-first-served creates daily stress
  • Wasted Spaces: Assigned spots empty when employees are on leave or WFH
  • Visitor Hassles: Clients circling for parking creates poor first impressions
  • EV Dilemma: Growing demand for charging with limited infrastructure

Visitor Parking Management

First impressions matter. Our visitor parking module ensures smooth experience:

👥 Visitor Flow

  • Pre-Registration: Employees register visitors in advance
  • Automatic Allocation: System assigns visitor parking spot
  • Entry Link: Visitor receives SMS/email with parking details and entry code
  • Seamless Entry: Number plate recognition or code entry at gate
  • Host Notification: Employee notified when visitor arrives

EV Charging Management

As electric vehicles become common, managing charging infrastructure is critical:

  • Charger Booking: Reserve EV charging slots along with parking spot
  • Time-Based Allocation: Fair allocation with time limits to serve more users
  • Charging Status: Real-time visibility of charging progress through app
  • Completion Alerts: Notifications when charging is complete to vacate spot
  • Usage Analytics: Plan infrastructure expansion based on demand data

Infrastructure Integration

Our platform integrates with parking infrastructure:

  • ANPR Cameras: Automatic number plate recognition for contactless entry
  • Boom Barriers: Automated opening for registered vehicles
  • Occupancy Sensors: Real-time slot availability from IoT sensors
  • Digital Signage: Guidance displays showing available spots
  • Access Control: Integration with building security systems

Allocation Policies

⚙️ Configurable Policies

  • Grade-Based Priority: Senior employees get first access to limited spots
  • Carpool Incentives: Premium spots for employees who carpool
  • EV Priority: Dedicated spots for electric vehicles
  • Accessibility: Reserved spots for employees with disabilities
  • Fairness Rules: Rotation policies to ensure equitable access

Parking Analytics

Make data-driven decisions about parking infrastructure:

  • Peak utilization times and days
  • No-show rates and patterns
  • EV charging demand trends
  • Visitor parking load analysis
  • ROI calculation for infrastructure investment
