Fleet Management with AFM, ASTI & Imprint: Enterprise-Grade Solutions

September 29, 2026 10 min read

Managing a large fleet of vehicles is one of the most complex operational challenges facing enterprises today. From tracking thousands of assets across geographies to ensuring driver compliance and optimizing fuel consumption, fleet managers need powerful tools that provide complete visibility and control. Shiksha Infotech's comprehensive fleet management ecosystem – featuring AFM, ASTI, and Imprint – delivers exactly that.

The Three Pillars of Fleet Excellence

Our fleet management platform is built on three integrated solutions, each addressing critical aspects of fleet operations:

🎯

AFM - Asset Fleet Management

Complete asset lifecycle management from procurement to disposal with real-time tracking and maintenance scheduling.

🧠

ASTI - Smart Fleet Intelligence

AI-powered analytics for route optimization, predictive maintenance, and operational insights.

👤

Imprint - Driver Management

Comprehensive driver profiling, behavior monitoring, compliance tracking, and performance analytics.

AFM: Asset Fleet Management

AFM provides end-to-end visibility into your entire vehicle fleet, regardless of size or geographic spread:

Real-Time Asset Tracking

  • GPS-based Location: Track every vehicle's position in real-time with 10-second refresh intervals
  • Geofencing: Create virtual boundaries and receive alerts when vehicles enter or exit designated areas
  • Historical Playback: Review complete trip history with stop analysis and route visualization
  • Multi-Map Support: Choose between Google Maps, HERE Maps, or custom map providers

Maintenance Management

  • Automated service reminders based on mileage, time, or engine hours
  • Digital maintenance logs with complete service history
  • Vendor management for service providers and spare parts
  • Warranty tracking and claim management

ASTI: Intelligent Fleet Operations

ASTI brings artificial intelligence to fleet management, transforming raw data into actionable insights:

🤖 AI-Powered Features

  • Predictive Maintenance: AI algorithms analyze vehicle health data to predict failures before they occur
  • Route Optimization: Machine learning optimizes routes based on traffic patterns, delivery windows, and vehicle capacity
  • Fuel Analytics: Identify fuel theft, inefficient driving patterns, and opportunities for cost reduction
  • Demand Forecasting: Predict fleet utilization needs based on historical patterns and business cycles

Operational Intelligence Dashboard

ASTI's dashboard provides fleet managers with:

  • Real-time fleet health scores and alerts
  • Customizable KPI tracking and benchmarking
  • Automated report generation and scheduling
  • Integration with business intelligence tools

Imprint: Driver Management Excellence

Your fleet is only as good as the people operating it. Imprint ensures driver safety, compliance, and performance:

Driver Profiling & Onboarding

  • Digital driver profiles with document management
  • Background verification integration
  • License validity tracking and renewal alerts
  • Training records and certification management

Behavior Monitoring

  • Driving Score: Composite score based on speeding, harsh braking, acceleration, and cornering
  • Fatigue Detection: Monitor driving hours and mandatory rest compliance
  • Distraction Alerts: Integration with in-cab cameras for mobile phone usage detection
  • Coaching Tools: Targeted training recommendations based on behavior analysis

Seamless Integration

Our fleet management ecosystem integrates with your existing enterprise systems:

✅ Integration Capabilities

  • ERP Systems: SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics integration for financial and operational data sync
  • Fuel Cards: Automatic reconciliation with HPCL, BPCL, IOCL, and private fuel networks
  • Telematics: Support for 50+ GPS device manufacturers and OBD-II protocols
  • HR Systems: Driver data sync with Workday, SuccessFactors, and custom HRMS
