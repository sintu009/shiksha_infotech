Transporter & Vendor Governance: B2B Fleet Management Excellence

September 29, 2026 8 min read

For enterprises relying on third-party transport vendors, managing multiple suppliers, ensuring service quality, and maintaining compliance is a significant challenge. Manual processes lead to billing disputes, SLA ambiguity, and inconsistent service quality. Shiksha Infotech's Transporter Governance platform provides a comprehensive B2B solution that brings transparency, accountability, and efficiency to vendor relationships.

The Vendor Management Challenge

Organizations working with transport vendors face recurring issues:

  • Multiple Vendors: Managing 10-50 vendors with different contracts and rates
  • Billing Disputes: Reconciling vendor invoices with actual services delivered
  • SLA Monitoring: Tracking whether vendors meet agreed service levels
  • Compliance Gaps: Ensuring vehicles and drivers meet regulatory requirements
  • Performance Visibility: Comparing vendor performance objectively

SLA Monitoring & Performance

Track vendor performance against agreed service levels:

Key SLA Parameters

  • On-Time Performance: Percentage of trips starting/ending on schedule
  • Vehicle Availability: Response to trip requests and breakdown recovery
  • Safety Score: Incidents, violations, and employee feedback
  • Compliance Rate: Document validity and regulatory adherence
  • Customer Satisfaction: Employee ratings and complaints

📊 Performance Dashboard

  • Real-time SLA scorecards for each vendor
  • Trend analysis over time
  • Comparative ranking across vendors
  • Automatic penalty calculations based on SLA breach
  • Monthly/quarterly vendor review reports

Compliance Tracking

Ensure all vehicles and drivers meet regulatory and organizational requirements:

Vehicle Compliance

  • Registration certificate validity
  • Insurance policy coverage and expiry
  • Fitness certificate status
  • Permit validity for commercial operation
  • Pollution certificate compliance

Driver Compliance

  • License validity and type verification
  • Police verification status
  • Medical fitness certification
  • Training and certification records
  • Violation and incident history

🔔 Automated Alerts

  • 30/15/7-day advance alerts before document expiry
  • Automatic blocking of non-compliant vehicles/drivers
  • Escalation to vendor admin for pending renewals
  • Compliance status reports for audit readiness

Billing & Reconciliation

Eliminate billing disputes with automated reconciliation:

How It Works

  1. Trip Recording: All trips logged with GPS-verified distance and time
  2. Rate Application: System calculates cost based on contract rate card
  3. Vendor Invoice Upload: Vendor submits invoice through portal
  4. Auto-Reconciliation: System matches invoice against calculated amount
  5. Variance Flagging: Discrepancies highlighted for review
  6. Approval Workflow: Multi-level approval for payment processing

Vendor Self-Service Portal

Empower vendors with their own portal:

  • Trip Management: View assigned trips and update status
  • Fleet Updates: Add/remove vehicles and update documents
  • Driver Management: Maintain driver database and assignments
  • Invoice Submission: Upload invoices and track payment status
  • Performance Review: Access their SLA scores and feedback

Vendor Analytics

Make data-driven decisions about your vendor portfolio:

  • Cost comparison across vendors for similar routes
  • Performance trends and improvement tracking
  • Capacity utilization by vendor
  • Risk assessment based on compliance and performance
  • Recommendations for vendor consolidation or expansion
