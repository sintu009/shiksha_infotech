September 29, 2026 7 min read

Employee safety during commute isn't just a regulatory requirement—it's a fundamental responsibility that defines organizational culture. With increasing incidents of transport-related safety concerns, especially for employees working late shifts, enterprises need robust safety mechanisms that go beyond basic tracking. Shiksha Infotech's comprehensive safety solution ensures peace of mind for employees, their families, and organizations.

Comprehensive Driver Verification

Every driver in our network undergoes rigorous verification before being assigned to employee transport:

🔍 Verification Checklist

  • Police Verification: Background check through official police records
  • Address Verification: Physical verification of driver's residential address
  • License Validation: Real-time validation with RTO database
  • Medical Fitness: Periodic health check-ups and fitness certificates
  • Drug Testing: Random drug and alcohol testing protocols
  • Behavioral Assessment: Psychological screening for temperament evaluation

Continuous Monitoring

Verification isn't a one-time activity. Our system continuously monitors:

  • License validity and renewal status
  • Traffic violation records
  • Employee feedback and ratings
  • Driving behavior patterns

SOS & Emergency Response

When an employee feels unsafe, every second counts. Our SOS system ensures rapid response:

Multi-Channel SOS Triggers

  • App Button: One-tap SOS button in employee mobile app
  • Shake Gesture: Vigorous phone shake triggers silent SOS
  • Voice Command: Hands-free SOS activation with voice keyword
  • In-Vehicle Button: Physical panic button in equipped vehicles

Response Protocol

✅ When SOS is Triggered

  1. Instant Alert: Control room receives alert within 5 seconds
  2. Location Capture: Exact GPS coordinates captured and logged
  3. Audio Recording: Automatic audio recording begins
  4. Contact Cascade: Emergency contacts notified automatically
  5. Response Dispatch: Nearest security personnel or police alerted
  6. Live Monitoring: Dedicated operator monitors situation until resolved

Women Employee Safety Compliance

We ensure 100% compliance with government regulations for women employee transport:

  • Guard Assignment: Security guard mandatory for trips starting/ending after 8 PM
  • First-In-Last-Out: Women employees picked up first and dropped last
  • Verified Female Drivers: Option for female-only driver assignments
  • Designated Safe Stops: Pre-approved, well-lit pickup/drop locations
  • Control Room Monitoring: Enhanced monitoring for night shift transport

Safety Technology Stack

Our safety features are powered by cutting-edge technology:

  • AI-Based Anomaly Detection: Machine learning identifies unusual patterns
  • Face Recognition: Driver identity verification before trip start
  • In-Cab Cameras: Optional video monitoring with privacy controls
  • Fatigue Detection: AI monitors driver alertness during long trips
  • Geofencing: Automatic alerts for entering restricted or unsafe zones
